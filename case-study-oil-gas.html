// THE STRATEGIC MANDATE

Defending Market Share Through Digital Agility.

ExxonMobil faced a critical defensive challenge: regional fragmentation and manual "back-office" bottlenecks were significantly slowing time-to-market. Branded wholesalers and distributors operated under disconnected procurement tools, creating manual friction that threatened billions in annual commerce.

The mandate wasn't merely a UI refresh. It was a comprehensive re-engineering of global commerce operations: standardizing complex regional tax jurisdictions, right-to-left localization, and order approval workflows into a single responsive digital platform.

Cross-Border Compliance

Navigated dynamic tax laws and localized customs protocols across 25 languages, ensuring 100% adherence to international fuel distribution standards.

Risk Safeguards

Engineered context-aware validation gates preventing multi-million dollar volume mismatches and inventory routing errors before order confirmation.

Operational Command Center Interface

// SYSTEMS DESIGN

The Rules Engine: Automating High-Value Logic

At the heart of the platform was a rigorous back-office rules engine that transformed manual multi-stage approval processes into a continuous, real-time experience.

High-Density Command Center

Consolidated 15+ disparate ERP and logistics data feeds into a unified console for regional order analysts, cutting cognitive overload by over 50%.

Automated Self-Service

Empowered international distributors to manage billion-dollar wholesale contracts, contract balances, and carrier nominations autonomously.

Localization & RTL Architecture

Built dynamic layout primitives that mirrored interfaces seamlessly for right-to-left scripts (Arabic, Hebrew) while maintaining component fidelity.

User Journey & Process Orchestration

Mapping buyer, distributor, and compliance manager touchpoints across order stages.

Global Information Architecture

Modular taxonomy accommodating varying regional product catalogues and tax guidelines.
