// THE BOTTLENECK

Eradicating Blindspots in High-Velocity Auto Parts Yards

In the automotive aftermarket, receivers and parts dealership managers were unable to view the specific contents of incoming freight containers before they arrived at the dock. This created severe staging bottlenecks, stock disorganization, and accounting ledger discrepancies.

Additionally, QA inspections repeatedly ground fulfillment lines to a halt. The objective was to design a comprehensive system providing complete parts traceability from inbound container arrival to technician dispatch.

Automated defect inspection alerts during receiving, routing damaged components to return queues without stalling healthy pallets.
