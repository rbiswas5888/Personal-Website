// CONTEXT & SCALE

Ecosystem Pressures in a Multi-Service Super App

Tata Pay operates inside India's high-speed UPI rails, where consumers demand instantaneous sub-second confirmation, bank-grade trust cues, and effortless failure recovery. Within Tata Neu, grocery (BigBasket), electronics (Croma), fashion (Tata CLiQ), and travel (Air India) all compete for checkout priority.

The core issue was not simply adding UPI. It was resolving how to make financial behavior trustworthy, transparent, and consistent when users navigate multiple linked bank accounts, UPI mandates, and rewards points in a single transaction.

TRUST BARRIER

Users evaluate every screen against dedicated bank app stability. Any visual hesitation degrades checkout conversion.

RECOVERABILITY

NPCI bank downtime shouldn't feel like an app crash. Failures must articulate whether funds moved and next steps.

// STRATEGIC EXECUTION

Key Architectural Decisions

Every design choice eliminated cognitive load, reduced decision fatigue, and prioritized emotional reassurance during payment execution.

DECISION 01

Intent-Led Hierarchy Over Banking Jargon

Re-architected the main surface around 4 direct consumer intentions: Send, Scan & Pay, Request, and Manage Accounts—rather than complex account terminology.

Outcome: ~40% reduction in initial navigation steps.
DECISION 02

Persistent Multi-Bank State Transparency

Users frequently manage multiple linked bank accounts. We designed persistent active-state pills and seamless 1-tap switching without having to exit or restart checkout journeys.

Outcome: ~25% higher task confidence during repeat payments.
DECISION 03

Actionable Failure States as Product Moments

Treated failures as critical product moments rather than abrupt dead ends. Built clear timing, status reassurance ("No funds deducted"), and smart automated retry prompts.

Outcome: ~30% improvement in user comprehension and reduced duplicate charges.
DECISION 04

Scalable Tokens Across Super App Verticals

Standardized tokens for biometric prompts, bank badge chips, and numeric keypads, facilitating fast expansion into lending, gold, insurance, and bill payments.

Outcome: ~20% faster sprint delivery for downstream checkout integration.

FLOW 02 · CONSTRAINTS & COMPLIANCE

Balancing Speed with Strict NPCI Compliance

Every simplification had to comply with RBI regulations and NPCI guidelines. We negotiated micro-interactions where regulatory text was delivered with visual clarity rather than legal clutter, maintaining swift user velocity.
